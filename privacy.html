Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 12, 2026. This policy covers the apps TaiPa, SkillBrowser, and MediKey by tmpacc100. Where the apps differ, the difference is stated explicitly below.

What we store

Your content (focus sessions, bookmarks, settings, usage learning) is stored on your device only. We do not run servers that collect your personal data, and we have no account system.

SkillBrowser

SkillBrowser contains no advertising and no analytics. It does not link the AdSupport or AppTrackingTransparency frameworks, does not read the advertising identifier (IDFA), does not collect a Device ID or Advertising Data, and does not track you across other companies' apps and websites. The app's catalog ships inside the app, so it makes no network requests of its own; the only network activity is Apple's own App Store purchase handling. Its privacy manifest declares NSPrivacyTracking = false with no tracking domains and no collected data types.

TaiPa and MediKey

The free tier shows rewarded ads via Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as the IDFA) and usage data to serve and measure ads. We request App Tracking Transparency permission first; if you decline, ads are shown non-personalized and the advertising identifier is not used. Ad content is limited to a general audience rating. See Google's policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple. We do not receive your payment details.

Your choices

In apps that show ads, you can change tracking permission at any time in iOS Settings. You can delete all local data by removing the app.

Children

These apps are not directed at children under 13.
