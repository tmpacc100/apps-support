Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026. This policy covers the macOS menu-bar apps Wakeful, Skylet, Rately, Untill, and Pausely.

Data we collect

None. These apps do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. There is no account system and no analytics or tracking.

On-device data

Your settings and content (such as saved locations, tracked rates, events, reminders, and preferences) are stored only on your Mac. Deleting the app removes them.

Network requests

Some apps fetch public information directly from third-party services to function: Skylet requests weather from Open-Meteo; Rately requests exchange rates from open.er-api.com and CoinGecko. These requests contain only what is needed (e.g. a city or currency) and no personal identifiers. Wakeful, Untill, and Pausely work fully offline.

Purchases

The optional one-time "Pro" upgrade is processed entirely by Apple via the App Store. We never receive your payment details.

Children

These apps are not directed at children and collect no data from anyone.
