Support

Help for the macOS menu-bar apps Wakeful, Skylet, Rately, Untill, and Pausely.

Common questions

  • Where does the app live? Each app runs in your menu bar (top-right of your screen). Click its icon to open it.
  • How do I restore my Pro purchase? Open the app's settings and tap "Restore purchase".
  • The app has no Dock icon. That's by design — these are lightweight menu-bar apps.
